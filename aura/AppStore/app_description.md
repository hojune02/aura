# aura App Description

aura is a focused breathing reset app for quick moments of calm before studying, coding, interviews, exams, focus blocks, and sleep routines.

Open the app, choose a guided reset, and follow a smooth breathing orb through inhale, hold, and exhale phases. aura includes useful free patterns, local stats, haptics, and premium options for deeper personalization.

Features:
- 60-second Focus Reset
- Box Breathing
- Guided animated breathing orb
- Local streaks and session stats
- Haptics for phase changes
- Calm visual themes
- Optional one-time premium unlock for extra patterns, custom sessions, additional themes, and detailed stats

aura is designed for focus and relaxation. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.
