Privacy Policy

aura does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores settings, completed-session records, streaks, and custom breathing patterns locally on your device. This data is not sent to a server.

Accounts and Analytics

aura does not require an account, does not include analytics, and does not use third-party tracking SDKs.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple's StoreKit system. Apple may process purchase-related information according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Health and Wellness

aura is designed for focus and relaxation. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.
