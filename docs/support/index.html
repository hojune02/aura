Purchases

aura Premium is a one-time non-consumable unlock handled by Apple's StoreKit system. If Premium does not appear after purchase, open Settings in the app and tap Manage aura Premium, then Restore Purchases.

Privacy

aura stores settings, stats, and custom patterns locally on your device and does not collect personal data.
